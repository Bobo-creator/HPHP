July 1, 2024

UAPB 🤝 U of A Fayetteville

University of Arkansas at Pine Bluff and the University of Arkansas at Fayetteville are working to combat food deserts at a local level through the HPHP. Local junior high school students are being taught to plant, maintain and harvest their own gardens. This experience has taught them to make predictions and observations and collect and analyze data. They have also learned how to raise fish and grow produce indoors through the use of aquaponics systems.

June 28, 2024

Design>>Teach>>Outreach

HPHP is aiming to build partnerships in the food desert community. Specifically, designing curriculum for programs at the PreK-12 level in the state of Arkansas. The curriculum focuses on teaching students how to grow and cook nutritional food for their families through gardening and healthy eating.
